# Seed Incidents: "Free Palestine" as Antisemitic Abuse

**Purpose:** Source document for generating `data/incidents.json`. Each incident has been confirmed by at least one credible source as explicitly involving the slogan "Free Palestine" (or a close variant) in the same act as antisemitic abuse, threats, or celebration of violence against Jews — or, in one case (Incident 14), as the declared name and banner of a group whose harmful act was carried out in direct advancement of the slogan.

**Coverage:** Australia (initial seed).

**Sources key:**
- ECAJ 2025 Report: https://www.ecaj.org.au/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/ECAJ-Report-Anti-Jewish-Incidents-Australia-2025.pdf

## Incidents considered but not included

The following were assessed and excluded because the "Free Palestine" slogan was not explicitly documented in the harmful act itself, even though other antisemitic elements were present or the broader context was pro-Palestinian.

**Opera House rally, Sydney, 9 October 2023.** A large pro-Palestinian gathering at the Opera House forecourt where antisemitic chants were directed at Jewish people. The documented chants are "where's the Jews" and "f*** the Jews" (the latter disputed by NSW Police forensic analysis). The event was a Free Palestine rally but the slogan itself does not appear in the documented harmful acts. Well-sourced: Times of Israel (https://www.timesofisrael.com/sydney-government-apologizes-for-pro-palestine-protest-that-had-gas-the-jews-chants/), ZFA (https://www.zfa.com.au/zfa-statement-in-response-to-nsw-police-determination/), Australian Jewish News (https://www.australianjewishnews.com/eleven-arrested-after-opera-house-fiasco-other-incidents/).

**Coogee motorcycle convoy, Sydney, 11 November 2023.** A pro-Palestinian motorcade from Lidcombe to Coogee, where about 100 Jewish people had gathered, described by the NSW Jewish Board of Deputies as "psychological warfare." Palestinian flags were displayed; specific "Free Palestine" chants directed at the Jewish gathering are not documented in available sources. Sources: The Jewish Independent (https://thejewishindependent.com.au/caulfield-and-coogee-a-new-chilling-phase-in-physical-hostility-to-jews/), Australian Jewish News (https://www.australianjewishnews.com/from-caulfield-to-coogee-hate-comes-to-our-doorstep/).

**Fortitude Valley, Brisbane, 31 May 2025.** Anti-Israel protesters at the Wickham Hotel threw an item at a Jewish man entering a religious event and came within 15 cm of his face shouting "baby killer" and "all Zionists are terrorists." A significant incident of targeted harassment; however, "Free Palestine" is not documented in the specific harmful acts recorded. Sources: ECAJ 2025 Annual Report (https://www.ecaj.org.au/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/ECAJ-Report-Anti-Jewish-Incidents-Australia-2025.pdf), Menzies tracker (https://www.menziesrc.org/news-feed/antisemitic-incidents-in-australia).

**Flaming projectile at rabbi and child, St Kilda East, 27 November 2024.** A flaming projectile was thrown from a moving car at a rabbi pushing a baby in a pram and a young boy on a scooter. Serious incident; no "Free Palestine" element documented. Source: Menzies tracker.

**Townsville tobacco shop, 23 November 2024.** Israeli backpackers called "dirty f***ing filthy Jew" by a pro-Palestinian tobacco shop employee. Employee described as "pro-Palestinian" but "Free Palestine" slogan not explicitly recorded. Source: Moment Magazine antisemitism monitor (https://momentmag.com/about-moment/antisemitism-monitor-2024/).
